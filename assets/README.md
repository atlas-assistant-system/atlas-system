# ATLAS Brand Assets

## Brand Overview

**ATLAS is a comprehensive personal assistant designed to centralize and simplify the management of everyday life, including schedules, routines, finances, health, wellbeing, beauty, personal goals, and other areas of daily life.**

Its purpose is to provide one central place from which the user can organize and manage their personal world.

---

## Design Explanation

### The Sphere

The circle at the center of the symbol represents the user's personal world — the totality of the areas that make up daily life. It is deliberately abstract; it is not a globe, not a planet, not a data visualization. It is a whole.

### The Internal Divisions

Three diameter lines cross the sphere at 60° intervals, creating six equal sectors. These divisions do not represent specific life areas through icons or labels. Instead they communicate the idea of a structured, organized world — a life partitioned into areas that are distinct but part of the same whole. The divisions use the cyan accent (#36C5F0) to visually separate them from the primary structure.

### The Support Structure

Two arms extend downward from the base of the sphere, spreading outward to form a stable base. A crossbar connects them at the midpoint. Together they form an abstract "A" shape — a subtle, structural reference to Atlas without depicting a human figure. This is the supporting geometry: Atlas holding up the world.

### The Blue Palette

- **Deep Navy (#0B1F3A)** — Authority, depth, trust. Used for typography on light backgrounds and as a brand ground.
- **Atlas Blue (#246BFD)** — The primary identity color. Modern, clear, and confident without being corporate.
- **Cyan Accent (#36C5F0)** — A restrained secondary tone used within the sphere to suggest variety within unity.

### Symbol and Wordmark

The symbol is designed to work completely independently from the wordmark. ATLAS in uppercase with generous letter-spacing paired with the "Personal Life Assistant" tagline completes the identity in context.

---

## Clear Space

Maintain a clear-space margin equal to the radius of the central sphere on every side of the logo. No other elements, text, or imagery should enter this zone.

---

## Misuse

Do not:
- Change symbol proportions or rearrange internal geometry
- Stretch or distort the logo
- Add unrelated colors, shadows, bevels, or 3D effects
- Add robot, AI, or neural-network imagery
- Turn the symbol into a detailed globe with continents
- Depict the mythological Atlas as a person
- Replace the wordmark with decorative typography
- Use the tagline below its minimum recommended size
